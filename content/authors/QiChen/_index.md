Qi Chen is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Adelaide. He received his Ph.D. in computer science from the University of Adelaide in 2024. Before that, he received a Master's degree from the School of Software Engineering at South China University of Technology, China, in 2020, and a Bachelor's degree from the same university in 2017. His research focuses on computer vision, multi-modality, and generative models. He serves as the reviewer for several top-tier conferences and journals including CVPR, ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, ICCV, ECCV, TPAMI, etc.
