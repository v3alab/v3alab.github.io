Ziyi Wang is a PhD student at the School of Computer Science, Adelaide University, supervised by A/Prof. Qi Wu and Dr. Xinyu Wang. He completed his Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at Ocean University of China and earned his Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the University of Adelaide. His current research focuses on Agentic Large Language Models in Interactive Virtual Environments, particularly exploring the perception, reasoning, decision-making, and adaptive capabilities of multimodal agents in interactive game and virtual environments.
